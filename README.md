# Pangea

A colorful geography game for Android, to test what you know about the world and learn the rest. In English or
French (follows the phone's language), offline, no ads, no account.

- **Mystery country**: one outline a day, six guesses. Each guess tells how far, which way and how warm you are.
  Keep your streak going, share your result.
- **Flags**, **Capitals**, **Shapes**: 60-second rushes with four choices, combos up to x5, and a list of what to
  review at the end. Countries you miss come back more often.
- **Pin it**: ten countries to place on a blank map, points by distance.
- **Atlas**: the map to explore, with a card for every country (capital, population, area), colored in as you
  find them.
- Every game can be played on the whole world or one continent.

197 countries: the UN members plus Vatican, Palestine, Kosovo and Taiwan. Borders from
[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain), font Plus Jakarta Sans (SIL OFL).
