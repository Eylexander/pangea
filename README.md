# Pangea

A geography game for Android. I wanted something to test what I know about the world and actually learn the rest,
so here it is. English or French, works offline, no ads, no account.

What's in it:

- **Mystery country**: one country outline a day, six guesses. Each guess tells you how far you are and in which
  direction. Keep the streak going.
- **Flags**, **Capitals**, **Shapes**: 60 seconds, four choices, combos if you chain right answers. At the end you
  get the list of what you missed. Those come back more often until you know them.
- **Pin it**: ten countries to find on the map.
- **Road trip**: get from one country to another by naming the countries in between.
- **Atlas**: the whole Earth to browse, with a card for every country. It fills in with color as you find them.

You can play on the whole world or a single continent, in Easy, Normal or Hard. Easy sticks to the countries most
people know; Hard brings in the tiny islands and microstates.

197 countries: the UN members, plus Vatican, Palestine, Kosovo and Taiwan.
